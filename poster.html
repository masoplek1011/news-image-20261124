Europe · Geneva

GUKESH v SINDAROV: GAME 1 OPENS IN GENEVA

Tue 24 Nov

Defending champion Gukesh, 20, meets challenger Javokhir Sindarov, 20, in Game 1 of the World Chess Championship at 15:00 CET in Cologny, Geneva — the youngest final in history, and the first between two players born in the 21st century. Sindarov has White in the opener of the 14-game match, worth $2.5 million, running to 12 December.

North America · Middle East

CANADA AND QATAR TO SIGN INVESTMENT PACT

Tue 24 Nov

Canada and Qatar are set to sign a foreign investment promotion and protection pact on Tuesday — a key step towards unlocking the Qatari investment promised since January but not yet realised.

East Asia · Seoul

SEOUL SHAKE-UP: FIRST WOMAN TO RUN SPY AGENCY

Mon 23 Nov

President Lee Jae Myung reshuffled his cabinet on Monday: Kang Kum-sil becomes the first woman to direct the National Intelligence Service, Lim Woong-soon takes foreign affairs, and Hong Ihk-pyo becomes unification minister.

South Asia · Ayodhya–Janakpur

SARYU-KAMALA EXPRESS ROLLS ACROSS THE BORDER

Mon 23 Nov

Prime Ministers Narendra Modi and Balendra Shah flagged off the Saryu-Kamala Express on Monday — a weekly, 17-coach train linking Ayodhya and Janakpur over 604 km — in a virtual ceremony from New Delhi, alongside a development package for Nepal.

Middle East · Gaza

GAZA A “TOXIC WASTELAND”, REPORT WARNS

Mon 23 Nov

An Oxfam and CEOBS report published on Monday finds more than 99% of Gaza shows indicators of toxic contamination, with 78% of its land affected — air, soil and water hazardous for decades to come.

Southeast Asia · Java

JAVA FACTORY FIRE: 24 DEAD, 30 STILL MISSING

Fire Sat 21 Nov Toll Mon 23 Nov

The death toll from Saturday’s fire at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory in Serang, Banten, was confirmed at 24 on Monday, with about 30 people still missing as the search goes on.

Europe · Lisbon

LISBON MARCHES IN THE RAIN OVER LIVING COSTS

Sat 21 Nov

Thousands marched through Lisbon in the rain on Saturday against the cost of living, housing and low pay. A follow-up protest is planned at parliament on Tuesday, as MPs hold the final vote on the 2027 budget.

Southeast Asia · Myanmar

MYANMAR CALLS ASEAN MOVE “UNFAIR”

Sat 21 Nov

Myanmar’s military government called ASEAN’s decision on its representation “unfair and imbalanced” on Saturday, as the bloc’s row over the junta’s seat drags on.

Africa · Benghazi

RUSSIAN CORVETTE DESTROYED IN BENGHAZI PORT

Sun 22 Nov

A Russian missile corvette, believed to be the Merkury of the Steregushchiy class, was destroyed by fire in the port of Benghazi, Libya, in a report carried on Sunday.
